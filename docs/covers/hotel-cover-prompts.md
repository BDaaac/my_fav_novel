# Промпты обложки «괴담 호텔 탈출기»

По образцу https://john.hartnup.uk/poster-prompts/: сверху текст «афиши» (что на обложке), затем блок стиля (визуальные черты, шрифт, палитра, композиция, настроение, чего избегать) и одно правило про сюжет: **один главный предмет, одна цельная сцена позади, всё остальное — надпись**.

Общий текст для всех вариантов (вставлять первым):

```
Korean web novel cover, vertical 2:3.
Title (the only large text, exactly): 괴담 호텔 탈출기
No other words, no author name, no publisher logo, no English.

The story: an old grand hotel. Behind each numbered guest-room door lies
another world facing its own apocalypse, ruled by an evil god. Guests go in,
survive, and look for a way back out. Room 105 is the rest room where monsters
cannot enter. Unknown room names appear as empty squares □□□.
Possible subjects (pick ONE and commit): a brass room key on a ribbon;
a half-open numbered door with another world behind it; the reception bell
on an empty front desk; a lone guest in a long corridor of identical doors;
a room-number plate reading □□□.
```

---

## 1. Penny Dreadful / обложка викторианского «романа ужасов»
Лучше всего подходит к теме: дешёвый ужас-сериал, один кричащий цвет.

```
Design this cover in Penny Dreadful / Sensation Novel Cover style (1840s–1890s).
Visual traits:
- dramatic engraved scene at the peak moment of crisis
- black line illustration over a single flat colour tint
- figures at extreme expressive angles — fleeing, recoiling, menacing
- heavy black hatching for shadow areas
- gaslight or candlelight as the dramatic light source
Typography: the Korean title in bold condensed woodtype-like Hangul, split across two lines (괴담 호텔 / 탈출기), set in a band above the scene.
Palette: black engraving on white with ONE garish tint — blood red.
Layout: full-bleed dramatic scene; title band on top.
Subject: a guest wrenching open a numbered hotel door while something enormous presses against it from the other side.
Mood: melodramatic, sensational, breathless, Victorian popular horror.
Avoid: ornamental borders, more than one colour, good taste, artificial yellowing.
```

## 2. German Expressionist Film Poster / «Калигари»
Кривые коридоры, гигантская архитектура, крошечный человек.

```
Design this cover in German Expressionist Film Poster style (1919–1933).
Visual traits:
- distorted angular hotel corridor with impossible perspective; doors lean
- raking diagonal shadows cutting across the composition
- a single small guest dwarfed by the overwhelming dark architecture
- high contrast — vast black areas with sharp white highlights
- tilted, oblique framing creating unease
Typography: angular, compressed Hangul for 괴담 호텔 탈출기, set at a slight angle into the architecture (as if painted over a door frame).
Palette: black, white, cold grey; one deep red accent (the number plate of one door).
Layout: architecture dominates, figure subordinate, title at top.
Mood: threatening, psychological, nightmare geometry.
Avoid: warmth, natural light, decorative ornament, artificial ageing.
```

## 3. Polish Poster School / метафора
Один сюрреалистичный образ вместо сцены.

```
Design this cover in Polish Poster School style (1950s–1980s).
Visual traits:
- painterly surreal image, metaphorical rather than literal
- bold colour fields, hand-painted texture
- sparse composition with one strong image
Subject: a huge brass hotel key whose bow is shaped like a doorway, and through that doorway a burning, ending world.
Typography: title small and quiet, clean Hangul in a corner.
Palette: deep red, ultramarine, ochre.
Mood: artistic, symbolic, unsettling.
Avoid: literal illustration, busy text, characters' faces.
```

## 4. Monochrome + Single Accent / типографика
Обложка-надпись, без картинки.

```
Design this cover in Monochrome + Single Accent style (contemporary).
Visual traits: no illustration, or one minimal mark (a tiny door outline or a key);
black and white dominance; strong negative space.
Typography: 괴담 호텔 탈출기 IS the image — huge, bold, carefully spaced Hangul;
one syllable or the door mark picked out in the accent colour;
a row of small empty squares □□□ as the only other element.
Palette: black, white, one accent — blood red.
Mood: confident, minimal, cold.
Avoid: illustration-heavy layouts, more than one accent colour.
```

## 5. Art Deco / парадный отель 1920-х
Отель как роскошь, которая прячет ужас.

```
Design this cover in Art Deco style (1920s–1930s), as a grand-hotel poster gone wrong.
Visual traits: geometric symmetry and stepped forms; sunburst fan behind the hotel facade;
metallic gold accents; strong vertical emphasis; elevator-dial and door motifs.
Subject: the symmetrical hotel facade at night — every window lit, one window dark and open.
Typography: high-contrast geometric Hangul with inline decoration, title at the top.
Palette: black, gold, cream, deep teal; one coral-red accent in the open window.
Layout: symmetrical tower composition.
Mood: luxurious on the surface, wrong underneath.
Avoid: cute decoration, more than one figure.
```
